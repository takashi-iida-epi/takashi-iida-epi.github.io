About

From health behaviors
to better prevention.

I am a physician and epidemiologist-in-training interested in chronic disease prevention. My research focuses on how health behaviors—particularly physical activity—shape long-term physical and mental health, and how causal inference and other epidemiologic methods can help us study these relationships more rigorously.

I work with wearable, survey, and real-world health data to study health behaviors and disease over time. By combining rigorous epidemiologic methods with clinical insight, I aim to generate evidence that can inform practical and equitable approaches to prevention.

ORIGINAL ARTICLE · CO-FIRST AUTHOR

Physical activity and recurrent fall risk in community-dwelling Japanese people aged 40–74 years: the Murakami cohort study

Kamimura S†, Iida T†, Watanabe Y, Kitamura K, Kabasawa K, Takahashi A, Saito T, Kobayashi R, Oshiki R, Takachi R, Tsugane S, Iki M, Sasaki A, Yamazaki O, Watanabe K, Nakamura K

Eur Rev Aging Phys Act · 2022 · 19:20

In a five-year cohort of 7,561 community-dwelling Japanese adults aged 40–74, moderate leisure-time physical activity was associated with a lower risk of recurrent falls, whereas higher non-leisure-time activity was associated with greater risk.
