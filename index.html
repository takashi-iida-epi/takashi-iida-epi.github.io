About

From everyday movement
to long-term health.

I am a physician and clinical researcher studying physical activity, causal inference, and chronic disease prevention. I am interested in how everyday movement patterns shape health over time—and how we can study those relationships more rigorously.

My work connects wearable and real-world data with modern epidemiologic methods. The goal is to translate complex longitudinal data into evidence that can support practical and equitable approaches to prevention.
